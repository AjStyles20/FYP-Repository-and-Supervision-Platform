Project workflow

  1. Propose a project title and problem statement.
  2. Review the supervisor’s proposal criteria and secure acceptance.
  3. Have the department log the approved topic.
  4. Track progress through supervisor meetings and agreed actions.
  5. Submit the final work, defend it, and complete the Head of Department sign-off.
  6. After the Head of Department signs off, the project and its shared files appear in the completed repository.

Recommended milestones

  • Topic selection and problem framing
  • Literature review and project plan
  • Implementation and testing
  • Draft report and presentation preparation
  • Defence and final archival
