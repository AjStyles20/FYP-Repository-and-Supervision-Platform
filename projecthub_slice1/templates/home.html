Discover Baze Project Hub

One place for the whole final-year project, kept by the department.

Students see what has already been done before choosing a topic, supervisors keep their meetings and decisions in one record, and the Head of Department signs off each completed project into a searchable archive.

  1. ProposeThe student drafts and submits a topic.
  2. ApproveThe supervisor accepts; the coordinator logs the signed form.
  3. SuperviseMeetings, milestones and deadlines are recorded.
  4. DefendThe final report is submitted and the defence recorded.
  5. ArchiveThe HOD signs off, and the work joins the repository.

Search past research

Browse projects the Head of Department has signed off, see who supervised them, and find the documented gaps waiting for a new student to continue.

Work with your supervisor

Draft and submit your proposal, request meetings in your supervisor's office hours, and keep track of deadlines and milestones.

A record you can trust

Approvals, meeting records and sign-offs are kept with who did what and when, so the history of every project can be checked.
